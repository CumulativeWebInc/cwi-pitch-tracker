Honest limits

  • Seed pitches come from CWI's own 2026-09-14 outreach wave and CWI's Spotify rescan log (2026-09-16 → 2026-10-01). VERIFIED means a rescan confirmed the placement; UNVERIFIED means the pitch is real but the outcome is unknown.
  • Win rates score verified outcomes only — pending pitches never inflate a curator's rate, and they never drag it down.
  • Positions shown are internal ops data. Per Lansky's rule they are never quoted in promo copy.
  • Do-not-resubmit flags are binding: Hype Off Life acknowledged the 9/14 form — no second send.
  • Anything you log here lives on this device only (localStorage), labeled USER-ENTERED, never presented as verified. The machine-readable seed is at pitches.json (schema cwi.pitch-record/1.0).
  • Placements go stale: the pipeline schedules a re-verify nudge 30 days after each placement's last check. An un-reverified placement is overdue, not a win.
